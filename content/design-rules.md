The design rules are the constrains and limits you impose for routing your PCB. This is something you adjust depending on the complexity that you need: high number of layers, impedance control, etc. or if it's for consummer electronics, medical contains high voltage, etc. These rules are tightly tied to the assembly constraints from the manifacturer. There are some standards that exist in some cases, and it is good practice to overview them before starting routing your PCB.
